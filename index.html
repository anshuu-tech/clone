Frequently Asked Questions

Key questions about venue booking, capacity, parking, and catering at Indra Marriage Garden Jhansi.

Where is Indra Marriage Garden located in Jhansi?

Indra Marriage Garden is located prominently on Bahar Unnao Gate Main Road, Near Naogoji Kua, Jhansi, Uttar Pradesh - 284002. It is easily accessible from all key localities across Jhansi city with broad road connectivity.

What is the guest capacity of Indra Marriage Garden?

Our expansive open lawn and covered dining pavilion can comfortably host events ranging from 300 to over 1,600+ guests for weddings, receptions, and large gatherings without overcrowding.

Is dedicated parking available for wedding guests?

Yes, Indra Marriage Garden offers convenient on-site parking along Bahar Unnao Gate main road, providing ample room for four-wheelers and two-wheelers for smooth arrival and departure.

Is there a covered dining area in case of seasonal rain?

Yes, we feature a fully covered dining pavilion that protects buffet lines, live counters, dessert stations, and dining guests from unexpected seasonal rain or sunlight.

How can I book or schedule a site visit?

You can call our manager directly at +91 98765 43210, send a message through the WhatsApp button on this site, or fill out the booking inquiry form above. Our venue manager is available 7 days a week from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM.
